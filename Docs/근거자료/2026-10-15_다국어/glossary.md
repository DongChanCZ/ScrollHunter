# Scroll Hunter localization glossary (ko → en → ja / zh-Hans / es)

Game: real-time roguelike card battle. The player (a mage-like "Adventurer") uses 4 cards in hand (Q/W/E/R),
reads enemy cast bars (green = normal attack, orange = stun attack, red = heavy attack halved by Shield, uninterruptible),
interrupts casts and gains Shield. Cost regenerates over time. Defeat lets you inherit one skill/passive.

## Fixed (never translate)
Keys and labels: Q, W, E, R, A, S, Shift, Esc, Space, HP, FPS, [Player]. Original English titles (Scroll Hunter, FOREST STAGE CLEAR, DESERT STAGE, COMING SOON...?) are not in this catalog.

## Style
- ja: UI in concise standard Japanese (です/ます for guidance/system messages, noun phrases for labels). Dialogue: Adventurer casual and calm (俺 is fine to AVOID — prefer no pronoun or 私/こっち sparingly; keep gender-neutral), thug and bandits rough (おい, てめえ), bandit leader gruff (貴様/お前), mage arrogant and cold (貴様, 〜だ, 〜ではないか). Use 「」 for quotes, …… for ellipses, full-width ！？ in dialogue.
- zh (Simplified only): concise mainland game UI style. Dialogue natural spoken Chinese, rough for bandits, arrogant for mage (你这家伙/区区…). Use “” for quotes, …… for ellipses, full-width punctuation ，。！？：；（）.
- es (neutral international Spanish, tú form): concise UI labels (buttons ideally ≤ English length + 30%); opening ¿ ¡; «» or "" for quotes; ellipsis "...". Use "coste". Avoid gendered references to the adventurer where natural.
